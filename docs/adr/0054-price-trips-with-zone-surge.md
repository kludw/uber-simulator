# 0054. Price trips with zone surge set by dispatch, declined by riders

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-08

## Context

Milestone 28 ([#294](https://github.com/kludw/uber-simulator/issues/294)) adds surge pricing, the first product feature past the original scope (`docs/spec.md` lists pricing as out of scope): when riders without a driver outnumber idle drivers in an area, prices rise, riders with a lower willingness to pay decline, the UI shows surge zones. It is a portfolio feature: the model must be easy to explain in a sentence each, not realistic economics.

Forces:

- Brains are pure and seeded (simulation skill); each concern gets its own child stream, labeled per tick (`patience:<tick>`). With surge off every output and event log must stay byte-identical.
- Dispatch already knows, exactly and per region, the two counts surge needs: its requested trips without a driver, and its idle drivers with their cells (ADR 0048). A trip belongs to its pickup's region (ADR 0050). Matching goes through offers (ADR 0018); riders cancel when patience runs out (120-300 ticks).
- The UI heatmap draws 5 × 5-cell tiles (ADR 0053). Regions are `columns × rows` equal tiles of the 500 × 500 grid (ADR 0050).
- Messages over core NATS can be lost to one subscriber (ADR 0028, 0041); there is no request/reply.

Spike on the unmerged branch `295-exp-surge` (`b3725e5`): the model below in process behind env knobs, `bun src/sim/spike-matrix.ts SPIKE_SURGE=on SPIKE_CAP=2`. ## Decision

We will let dispatch price **surge zones** from its own counts every 30 ticks, publish the prices, and let each rider compare its zone's price with its own **max surge** when it spawns: above it, the rider declines and leaves; otherwise it requests the trip at that price, fixed for the trip.

1. **Surge zones**: the grid in 50 × 50-cell squares (500 m; 10 × 10 on the spec grid), numbered row-major from 0, `zoneOf(grid, cell)` in one pure shared module (`src/shared/surge.ts`). A zone cut by a region border is priced per part, each part by its region's dispatch; layouts whose columns and rows divide 10 (`1x1`, `2x1`, `2x2`, `5x5`, `10x10`) cut none.
2. **Surge** (multiplier): every 30 ticks (tick % 30 = 0), after that tick's matching, each dispatch counts per zone its **unmatched trips** (state `requested`: queued or offered, no driver yet, by pickup) and its idle drivers (by cell), and sets `surge = unmatched / max(idle, 1)`, rounded to 0.1, clamped to [1.0, 2.0]. Twice as many unmatched trips as idle drivers is 2.0×, the cap. In sparse zones this is nearly a step: with no idle driver, 1 unmatched trip is 1.0× and 2 or more are 2.0×; values in between need idle drivers (e.g. 3 unmatched against 2 idle is 1.5×), which is the common case at scale (about 1,000 drivers per zone at 100k).
3. **Message `zones.priced`** (event, `sim.events.zones.priced`, unregioned subject: see below): `{ tick, region, zones: [{ zone, surge }] }`, the region's zones above 1.0 in zone order, one object per zone (at most 100 entries per region every 30 ticks, so the parallel arrays of ADR 0047, a hot-path measure for 5,000-entry moves, buy nothing here); published every pricing tick, empty when nothing surges (so a zone returning to 1.0 is seen). Zones not listed are 1.0. Zod shapes: `region` a `Region`; `zone` a non-negative integer (below the zone count, checked where the grid is known, like `cellIn`); `surge` a number in [1.0, 2.0] (here > 1.0) that is a whole number of tenths. Subject: `subjectFor` appends `.region-<k>` only for the ten regioned types of ADR 0050 (keyed on the type set `subscriptionSubject` already uses, no longer on the presence of a `region` field), so `zones.priced` goes on `sim.events.zones.priced` and riders, the UI and the persister subscribe to that one subject; no dispatch takes it. This changes no existing subject: today exactly those ten types carry `region`.
4. **Rider decision**: the rider service keeps the last `zones.priced` per region (a view). A spawned rider's **quote** is the surge of its pickup's (region, zone), 1.0 if none seen. Its **max surge** is uniform in [1.0, 3.0), drawn from the child stream `willingness:<tick>` (one draw per spawned rider, so declines never shift other draws). Quote above max surge: the rider publishes `rider.declined_surge { tick, riderId, pickup, surge }` and is gone (no trip; its trip ID `t-n` is never used). Otherwise `request_trip` carries `surge` (same Zod range as above, here ≥ 1.0).
5. **Fare** (integer cents): `round((250 + 2 × distance(pickup, dropoff)) × surge)`, i.e. $2.50 + $2 per km, times surge. Dispatch prices the request: `trip.requested` carries `surge` and `fare` (a positive integer), fixed for the trip (the quote is honored even if the zone's surge changed since). **Revenue**: the sum of fares of completed trips. Readers: the summary (`bun run sim`, `--compare-surge`) joins `trip.completed` to its `trip.requested` by trip ID, as it already does for ticks to pickup, and sums the event's `fare`; for a trip without one (surge off) it applies `fareOf` at 1.0×, so the off column of `--compare-surge` is the same trips at base fare. That is the only place the formula runs outside dispatch. `bun run report` sums the events' `fare` only (SQL over `payload`), for surge-on runs.
6. **Option** `--surge on|off` for `bun run sim` and `bun run bench`, `SURGE` for `bun run dev` and the processes (every process gets the same value, like `REGIONS`), default `off`. Off: dispatch publishes no `zones.priced`, riders draw nothing from `willingness:*` and add no `surge`, `trip.requested` has no `surge` / `fare`; every output and event log is byte-identical to before. `bun run sim -- --compare-surge` runs the configured matching with surge off and on, on one seed, side by side: trips requested, riders declined, completed, cancelled, mean ticks to pickup, revenue (off: same trips at base fare), invariant violations. Single runs with `--surge on` add "riders declined" and "revenue" lines.
7. **Drivers don't react**: no driver brain change (no repositioning toward surge, no surge share in pay).

## Rationale

- **Dispatch already has the numbers**, exact and current, per region: no new view, no extra decoding of `drivers.moved` (dispatch's dominant cost at scale, ADR 0050). Pricing per region part follows trip ownership, so no instance asks another.
- **The rider owns the decision**: willingness to pay is the rider's private value, so the rider brain draws it and decides, and a decline never touches dispatch or the trip lifecycle (no trip, no offer, no new transitions, no spec invariant changes). Price fixed at request keeps one simple rule: the price you saw is the price you pay.
- **A published price map instead of a quote round-trip**: one small message per region per 30 ticks serves riders, the UI and analytics alike, and needs no request/reply (ADR 0028) or pending state. Staleness is bounded by 30 ticks plus delivery, well under rider patience.
- **Explainable**: three numbers (50-cell zones, every 30 ticks, cap 2.0×) and one ratio. The plain ratio with cap 3.0 jumped 1 → 2 → 3 in sparse zones and mostly produced declines; capping at 2.0 with max surge up to 3.0 keeps at least half the riders willing at any price, and the measured outcome matches the softer formula with fewer words.
- **The effect is where it should be**: at spec load no zone ever surges (identical outcomes, one surged request in 2,264), so surge never punishes a city with spare drivers. Under overload it trades patience cancellations for up-front declines (heavy: −25% to −36% cancellations; 23-26% of spawned riders declined, 47-53% were quoted above 1.0× and about half of those declined; mean quote of requested trips 1.31-1.36×), completed trips stay supply-bound (−8% to +3%), mean ticks to pickup within 1 or lower, revenue +13% to +33%. In the busy city it bites mildly (2-5% of spawned riders declined, 3-11% quoted above 1.0×, revenue +1% to +3%, pickups 4 ticks slower), and batched loses 20 completions (−3%), likely because declines cluster at the downtown hotspot, whose short pickups batched pairs best; #296 re-measures with the real implementation.
- **50-cell zones**: about 1 idle driver per zone at the spec fleet and 1,000 at 100k, coarse enough to count something, fine enough to show downtown (radius 50) against the airport; 10 × 10 heatmap tiles per zone (ADR 0053), so the UI's tiles nest in zones; borders align with the common region layouts (250 is a zone border, 100 cells is not). 100-cell zones earned more but shed twice the demand and cut regions at `2x1`.
- **Every 30 ticks**: measured within noise of every 10 or 60; half a simulated minute reads as plausible and keeps messages rare.

## Alternatives considered

- **Riders send their max surge in `request_trip`, dispatch rejects above it**: no new message to riders, but dispatch would make the rider's choice with the rider's private value, and a declined trip becomes a `request_trip_rejected` reason plus a dispatch event for a trip that never existed. Rejected.
- **Quote round-trip** (`request_quote` → `quote` → `request_trip`): freshest price, but a command/reply pair, a pending rider state, and two more losable legs (ADR 0041) for freshness the 30-tick map already bounds. Rejected.
- **Price in `request_trip_accepted`, rider cancels if too high**: the trip enters the queue and may get an offer and a driver before the cancel lands; declines count as cancellations and churn drivers. Rejected.
- **A separate pricing service**: would rebuild unmatched trips and idle drivers from events dispatch already holds, including decoding every `drivers.moved` at 600k. Rejected.
- **Surge per region**: the default layout is one region, the whole city. **Per heatmap tile** (5 × 5): about 0.04 drivers per tile at the spec fleet, pure noise. Rejected.
- **Smoothing** (moving average per zone) or a softer formula: more state or words for a measured effect within noise of the plain ratio at cap 2.0. Rejected.
- **Drivers reposition toward surge** (or earn a surge share): the real supply-side effect, but it changes driver wandering and shifts, and needs its own measurement. Out of scope; a later milestone can add it.
- **A new ClickHouse table or columns for prices**: `payload` already holds `surge` and `fare` and the report reads payloads for one run. Rejected for now.

## Consequences

- **Messages**: new events `zones.priced` and `rider.declined_surge` (first rider event: `isSimEvent`, `SimEvent` and the subject kinds gain `zones` and `rider`; the persister stores both, `rider_id` filled for declines); optional `surge` on `request_trip`; optional `surge` and `fare` on `trip.requested`. Riders take one more type (`zones.priced`, ADR 0042) on its one unregioned subject. No dispatch input changes, no new regioned subject; `subjectFor` decides regioning by type, not by field (3).
- **Persistence and analytics**: no schema change. `bun run report` adds riders declined and revenue lines for runs with surge on (runs with any `zones.priced`); runs with surge off print as today.
- **UI** (#297): the view keeps the last `zones.priced` per region; the canvas tints surging zone parts (zone ∩ region bounds) by surge with a `1.4×` label in both draw modes; the panel adds surging zones, max surge, riders declined, revenue. The demo can pass `SURGE=on`.
- **Determinism**: pricing is a pure function of dispatch state at a tick; riders' declines depend on the seed and the `zones.priced` they have received. In process that order is fixed (same seed, same log); over NATS which update a rider has seen at a spawn varies, like every other cross-service order. Surge off draws nothing and publishes nothing new.
- **Regions**: each dispatch prices its own zone parts; riders key prices by (region, zone), and a trip's region is its pickup's (ADR 0050), so a quote always comes from the dispatch that will own the trip.
- **Message loss (ADR 0041)**: a lost `zones.priced` leaves a rider quoting a stale price until the next one (≤ 30 ticks), harmless; a lost `rider.declined_surge` reaches no service but the persister's stream. The price rides `request_trip`, so a lost request loses nothing new (today's path: the rider cancels, `unknown_trip`). No recovery mechanism needed.
- **Performance**: `zones.priced` is at most 100 entries per region per 30 ticks. Pricing is O(active trips + idle drivers) once per 30 ticks; the implementation counts idle drivers per zone inside `idle-drivers.ts` without collecting or sorting them (the spike's sort cost +6.7 ms at p95 at 200k). Riders add one map lookup and one draw per spawn. With surge off, one branch per tick. #297 spot-checks live greedy `1x1` at 600k with surge off and on.
- **Risks**: shed demand looks like lost trips in "completed" alone; reports show declines and revenue next to it. Revenue numbers are toy money (formula above).

Domain terms (domain skill, added with the code): **Surge** (multiplier on a fare, 1.0-2.0 in 0.1 steps), **Surge zone** (50 × 50-cell square, row-major; a zone cut by a region border is priced per part), **Pricing** (dispatch setting each zone's surge every 30 ticks, the **pricing interval**, from **unmatched trips**, `requested` trips without a driver, against idle drivers; not "waiting", which is a rider state and the UI's waiting riders), **Quote** (the surge a spawned rider sees for its pickup), **Max surge** (a rider's willingness to pay, uniform 1.0-3.0), **Declined** (a rider leaving because its quote exceeds its max surge, `rider.declined_surge`), **Fare** (cents, (250 + 2 × distance) × surge, fixed at request), **Revenue** (sum of completed trips' fares); events `zones.priced`, `rider.declined_surge`; drop "Pricing, surge" from "Not in scope yet".
